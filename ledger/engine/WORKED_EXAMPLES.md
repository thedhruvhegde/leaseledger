## Findings of the audit

- All recomputed lines match the engine to the cent (see 'ok' columns).
- Convention to explain: whole-unit rent is split over max(household, bedrooms), i.e. a solo student in a 5-bedroom house is priced as one of five housemates (all rooms filled); utilities are split the same way.
- Towers get $0 heating (central plant, assumed billed by the building) even when the listing does not say heat is included; electricity, internet and water are still charged.
- Travel is charged for every trip of every day tour: between-class trips (e.g. a North-Central-North day) are where homes differ most, not the home -> first-class trip.
