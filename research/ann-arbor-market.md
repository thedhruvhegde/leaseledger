### A2. Leasing calendar and timing law

- **Lease term norm:** 12-month leases running roughly Sept 1 to late Aug for student stock (Inference from listings "26/27 term", and the city's example of a lease ending Aug 28, H). University Northwood runs June to May (Verified).
- **Ann Arbor Early Leasing Ordinance plus Right to Renew, City Code §8:530** (Verified, H):
  - A landlord may **not show an occupied unit or sign a lease for the next term until 150 days before the current lease ends** (city example: lease ending Aug 28, 2024 → first date Mar 31, 2024).
  - Current tenants must be **offered renewal** (Right to Renew, ~6 months before lease end). Non-renewal requires written notice with good cause. Landlord may tell tenants next-term terms up to **240 days** before lease end.
  - Early Leasing and Right to Renew were merged and strengthened in **March 2024** (ORD-24-03, active ~Mar 14, 2024) to close loopholes.
  - **Loopholes reported** by the Michigan Daily: landlords holding units for future tenants before the 150-day window and running "voluntary" waitlists (Reported, M).
  - A citizen petition seeks to extend the window to 240 days; **not law** (Verified as petition only).
- **Practical effect (Reported, M):** even with the ordinance, the student market is described as front-loaded. A UM transfer student guide/Daily article says students typically have housing settled by **mid-October** for the following September, and a 2024 r/uofm thread says leases often start being signed in **November** the year before. A February admit is "months behind". The University Off-Campus Housing office, by contrast, says there is **no shortage** and spring or summer searchers still find housing. **These conflict:** popular units go early, but supply remains for later searchers. Treat both as true with different unit quality and price (Inference, M).

### A3. Fee law

- **§8:532 (effective Jan 26, 2025, passed Jan 6, 2025):** rental **application fees capped at $50**, refunded within 60 days if not offered a unit. **Bans pre-tenancy fees**, including waitlist, holding and option fees (Verified via city and union pages, H).
- **ORD-26-04 (adopted Feb 2, 2026, effective Aug 1, 2026):** rental **fee transparency** ordinance. The January 2026 introduction was described by WEMU as a ban on all mandatory fees. The adopted notice describes "transparency of rental fees". **Final operative scope NOT verified**: whether it bans or just requires disclosure of amenity/admin/tech fees (Verified that it was adopted, scope L).
- **Why it matters (Reported, H):** before §8:532, the Tenants Union and CBS Detroit reported waitlist fees near **$7,000** (Prime Student Housing, 2024-25) and **$6,745** for a 2BR (Campus Management). The Michigan Daily reported landlords renaming fees to evade rules.

### A4. Michigan security deposit law (Truth in Renting / Security Deposit Act, MCL 554.601 et seq.)

| Rule | Detail | Source/Status |
|---|---|---|
| Cap | Max **1.5 months' rent**. UM Off-Campus Housing notes any prepaid rent beyond the first month counts toward the cap | Verified (UM OCH, multiple legal guides), H |
| Move-in inventory | Landlord must give an inventory checklist. Tenant has **7 days** to complete and return it | Verified (FindLaw summary), H |
| Forwarding address | Tenant must give **written forwarding address within 4 days** after move-out (MCL 554.611). If not, landlord is excused from sending the damage notice, but the tenant can still claim the deposit. Exception: excused if landlord never gave the required deposit notice | Verified (Michigan Courts benchbook, statute), H |
| Return / itemized list | Within **30 days** after termination of occupancy, landlord returns deposit or mails an itemized list of damages | Verified, H |
| Tenant response | Tenant has **7 days** to respond in writing to the itemized list | Verified (multiple), M-H |
| Penalty | Up to **2x** amount wrongfully withheld, plus fees (per secondary sources) | Reported, M |
| Pending | HB 4992 (2025-26) would shorten the notice to 15 days and allow email/phone forwarding contact. **Not enacted** as of last seen status | Reported, M |

Free help: **UM Student Legal Services** does lease review and deposit disputes for students (Verified, H). City Rental Housing Services takes code complaints.

### A5. Energy and housing-quality regulation

- **Green Rental Housing Ordinance:** passed June 2025, **effective Jan 6, 2026**. Rentals must meet a minimum energy-efficiency level at inspection, and the city reports early compliance in ~500 units (Verified via city news release title and WEMU, H for existence, L for what the minimum is).
- City says renters have a right to a warm home, and the Housing Code requires basic weatherization (Verified, city sustainability page). **Exact minimum indoor temperature: NOT verified in this pass.**
- Washtenaw County environmental health: the majority of complaints are **mold and bedbugs** (Reported, M; date unknown).

### A6. Utilities structure

- Electricity and gas: **DTE Energy** is the utility (Verified, H). Water: City of Ann Arbor (Inference, H).
- High-rises typically bundle some utilities or use a **utility cap / overage** model. **Not verified in this pass** (no source found). This is an open research gap.
- Old houses: DTE says Michigan bills are high partly because of "a lot of older, large homes". A 2024 study attributed some of Ann Arbor's heating cost to old, poorly insulated stock (Reported, M).

### B4. Sublet market

- Strong May–Aug seasonal supply as students leave; guide claims sublets run 10–20% below lease rates (Reported, commercial guide, L).
- Original tenant stays liable if the subletter doesn't pay (Reported, M). Landlord consent often required, sometimes with full screening (Verified, UM Student Life parent page, M).
- r/uofm megathread (2023): many winter sublets are from people who failed to find fall-only subletters, and sublet scams target desperate searchers (Reported, M).
